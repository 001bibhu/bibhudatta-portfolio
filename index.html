An independent project I conceived, designed, and developed: a documentation and content management platform bringing together rich-text authoring, Docs-as-Code workflows, collaboration, publishing, and AI-ready content architecture.

Why I started building it

Years of working with documentation teams showed me how disconnected authoring, reviews, content reuse, developer workflows, and publishing can become. ProductHelp AI is my attempt to bring these experiences together in one product.
